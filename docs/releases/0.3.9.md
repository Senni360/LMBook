# LMBook 0.3.9

Ink now extends to the desktop and the controls you open while working.

- A custom draggable title bar includes the notebook title, app menu and minimize, maximize/restore and close controls. Open the menu with its button or F10. Mac controls sit on the left; its system menu remains available.
- Dropdown menus, confirmations and tooltips share paper surfaces, pen focus and brief opening motion. Selects retain their form and keyboard behavior.
- Audio chapters, source recordings and previews use matching playback, seeking and volume controls. Sliders, progress indicators and disclosure carets share Ink styling.
- Closing with active work uses an in-app choice to stay, continue in the background or cancel and quit. File dialogs and emergency errors still use the operating system where needed.
- A metadata-listener edge case no longer prevents playback position restoration during development remounts.

Reduced motion and high-contrast settings remain supported. This local version has not been published; native Mac execution of the new title bar remains pending. See [the visual and behavior evaluation](../ui/ink-desktop-controls.md).
